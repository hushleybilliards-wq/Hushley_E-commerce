## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-stakes, master-craftsman digital atelier for championship-tier billiards accessories. It merges the focused serenity of a darkened arena arena table with the tactile precision of bespoke cue manufacturing. The visual direction blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile / Technical Skeuomorphic** nuances—deep carbon textures, razor-sharp technical specifications, and deliberate metallic reflections.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning cueists, competitive tournament players, collectors, and bespoke cue artisans who value micro-millimeter tolerance, structural carbon rigidity, and prestige provenance.
- **Emotional Response:** Quiet confidence, uncompromising tournament readiness, tactile precision, and elite exclusivity. Every interaction mirrors unscrewing an aerospace-grade joint pin or testing tip density under stadium spotlights.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian foundation illuminated by tournament baize green and precision-milled gold accents.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Obsidian Dark (Base Canvas):** `#090A0F` provides absolute depth, simulating the shadows beyond the table lights.
- **Matte Carbon (Surfaces & Cards):** `#12141C` creates crisp, elevated structural panels.
- **Weave Boundary (Borders & Dividers):** `#222634` sets fine, low-contrast structural framing with faint carbon-grain undertones.
- **Tournament Emerald (Primary Accent):** `#10B981` denotes active states, live inventory indicators, verified certifications, and core interactive moments, referencing pristine tournament baize.
- **Master-Craft Gold (Secondary & Value Highlight):** `#EAB308` and `#F59E0B` signify premium specifications, pro-signature tiers, zero-COD compliance notices, and exclusive additions.
- **Text & Reading Hierarchy:**
  - High-Emphasis: `#F8FAFC` (pure chalk white for titles, vital metrics).
  - Mid-Emphasis: `#94A3B8` (technical spec labels, secondary descriptions).
  - Low-Emphasis: `#475569` (disabled states, inactive joint types, table metadata).

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural edge of **Syne** for headlines with the ergonomic clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces and dense technical catalogs.

### Typographic Rhythm & Execution
- **Display & Headlines (Syne):** Set with tight tracking to convey machined balance. Headers remain architectural, providing strong presence without distracting from product photography.
- **Body & Specs (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Tuned for instant legibility in high-density spec blocks (tip diameters, carbon wall thickness, deflection tables).
- **Technical Meta (`label-technical`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic laser-etched serial numbers and tournament cue shaft branding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, condensing to 8 columns on tablet devices and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoint Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem), compact `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem). Product grids collapse into a high-density vertical cadence with sticky spec drawer controls.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem margin, 1rem gutter. Catalogs display in 2-column balanced layouts.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, 3rem margin, 1.5rem gutter. Max-width constraint anchored at 1440px to ensure catalog rows, shaft comparison matrices, and spec badges maintain consistent visual alignment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers**, **Subtle Surface Sheens**, and **Low-Contrast Micro-Borders**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Base Level (`#090A0F`):** Canvas backdrop. Devoid of light; absorbs visual weight.
- **Surface Tier 1 (`#12141C`):** Standard product cards, navigation bars, spec panels. Encased in a `1px` border of `#222634`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (`#1A1D27`):** Hovered cards, flyout shopping carts, active dropdowns. Accompanied by an ambient spotlight shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
- **Accent Glow (Focus & Selection):** Activated elements receive a calibrated `0 0 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` or gold equivalent, evoking the rim-lit glow of cue balls under tournament arena halogen lamps.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a precise, engineered shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Corners maintain a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) micro-radius, with cards and modals utilizing `0.5rem` (8px).

This restrained curvature avoids bulbous aesthetics, reflecting the milled aerospace carbon fiber and turned exotic hardwood surfaces characteristic of championship cue craft.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Championship Buy / Checkout):** Deep carbon base or Tournament Green (`#10B981`) fill with dark contrast text (`#090A0F`), font `label-lg`, uppercase, subtle gold hover ring (`1px` inset `#EAB308`).
- **Secondary (Customizer / Joint Selector):** Translucent obsidian base with `1px` `#222634` outline. On hover: border transitions to `#10B981` with crisp white text.
- **Technical Action:** Minimized height, mono-spaced feel, using `label-technical`.

### Spec Badges & Chips
- **Tip Diameter (e.g., 11.8mm, 12.4mm, 12.8mm):** Tight technical badges with `#12141C` background and fine `#222634` perimeter border. Text in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Hardness Indicators (Soft, Medium, Hard):** Segmented pill chip with three-stage graduation. Active grade highlighted in `#10B981` (Soft/Medium) or `#EAB308` (Hard / Break Tip).
- **Joint Type (Radial, Uni-Loc, 3/8x10, Wavy):** Precision icon accompanied by bold technical acronym.
- **Carbon Taper (Pro Taper / Conical):** Subtle graphic contour indicator alongside metric text.

### Catalog Cards
- **Structure:** Carbon matte foundation (`#12141C`) wrapped in `#222634`. Top-right anchors the zero-COD indicator or limited tournament run tag.
- **Media Container:** High-contrast, rim-lit photography against deep charcoal backdrops.
- **Data Footer:** Spec matrix grid showing diameter, weight range, and joint thread directly accessible without opening quick-view modals.

### Policy & Status Indicators
- **Zero-COD Policy Banner / Badge:** Styled with Master-Craft Gold (`#EAB308`), featuring a micro padlock/vault icon and crisp text: `PRE-PAID SECURED ONLY • ZERO-COD PROTOCOL`. Ensures trust and clear transactional expectations.
- **Cart Badge:** Fixed counter with high-contrast `#10B981` background and pitch-black numerical text (`#090A0F`), keeping item counts instantly recognizable against the dark header.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** `#090A0F` inset surfaces with `#222634` borders. Active state triggers a 1px `#10B981` outline with zero exterior blur expansion to keep lines strictly rectilinear.
