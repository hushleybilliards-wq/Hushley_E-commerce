# Hushley Website: Quick Guide

On a phone, use the buttons at the bottom to open the shop, cart, orders, and profile. Staff can use the bottom buttons in the admin dashboard to reach the main management sections.

## For customers

### Create an account or sign in

1. Tap **Profile** and choose **Create an account**.
2. Enter your Gmail address and request a code. New accounts cannot use an address containing a `+` sign.
3. Enter the code sent to your email, then complete your name, mobile number, and password.
4. To return later, tap **Profile** and sign in. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and follow the email instructions.

### Shop and place an order

1. Tap **Shop** and browse or search for an item.
2. Open the item to see its description and choose any required options, such as size or hardness.
3. Tap **Add to cart**, or choose **Buy now** to go directly to checkout.
4. Open **Cart**, check the quantities, and select the items you want to buy. Tap **Continue to checkout**.
5. Sign in if asked. Check your contact and delivery details, choose a courier and payment method, then place the order.

### Pay and follow your order

1. After checkout, your new order opens under **My Orders**. To return later, open **Orders** and find it there.
2. Select **Upload payment proof** and follow the order's payment instructions. For the current process, pay for the products first and upload a clear picture of your payment proof.
3. Wait for the shop to check and approve your payment. Staff will contact you separately about the delivery cost and payment.
4. Return to **Orders** to check the status and any courier tracking number.
5. When the order arrives, tap **Order received** if the option is available. If it is unavailable, contact the shop.

An unpaid order may be cancelled once before payment proof is submitted: open the order, choose **Cancel order**, and give a reason. If you have paid or sent proof, contact the shop. No refund is sent automatically.

For older orders, follow the payment instructions shown on that order. Contact the shop if anything is unclear.

## For the shop owner and staff

### Open the dashboard

Sign in with the authorized shop owner account, then choose **Admin dashboard** or open `/admin`. Use the dashboard to manage the shop; use a separate customer account to place customer orders.

### Add or update products

1. Open **Products** and choose **Add product**.
2. Enter the name, category, price, stock, photo, description, and shipping size.
3. Add choices such as size or hardness if customers need them.
4. Select **Publish product to the shop** when it is ready, then save.
5. To change an item later, find it under **Products**, tap its edit icon, make your changes, and save. Use **Save stock** when changing its quantity.

You can also add a category from the **Products** page. An item needs a photo and description to appear in the shop.

### Review an order and payment

1. Open **Orders** or **Payments** and select the order.
2. Check the items, customer details, payment method, and payment proof.
3. Check the shop's bank or e-wallet account to make sure the money arrived.
4. Choose **Approve payment** only after confirming the payment. If it cannot be confirmed, reject the proof and contact the customer.

Payment is checked by staff. The website does not verify it automatically.

### Arrange delivery and finish the order

1. After approving the product payment, contact the customer about the delivery cost and payment.
2. Record the shipping details and update the order as it is prepared and sent.
3. Enter the tracking number provided by the courier when available.
4. For Lalamove in Luzon, the customer pays the rider directly. For international orders, explain that shipping is an estimate; privately arrange any refund or extra payment if the actual cost differs.

Use **More** in the mobile dashboard to open Shipping, Customers, Analytics, or the shop. Sign out when you finish on a shared device.
